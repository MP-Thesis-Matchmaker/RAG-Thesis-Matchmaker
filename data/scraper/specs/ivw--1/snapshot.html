Bachelor's Program Economics

Assessment Level Bachelor OEC (first and second semester)

In the first year of your studies you will get to know the entire range of all economic sciences (business administration, economics, mathematics, statistics, empirical economic research, finance). You will familiarize yourself with economic methodology and acquire the necessary basic knowledge in mathematics and statistics.

Advanced Level Bachelor (third semester and onward)

The Bachelor's program in economics teaches students methods for examining economic problems at micro and macro levels. With the knowledge you acquire you will be able to analyze these problems and express views on them. In the economics program you will acquire the analytical tools necessary to do this and learn to apply empirical methods.

You can find all information about the Bachelor's program in Economics in our  flyer (PDF, 572 KB).
