Theses

General Information

The BDLT offers various types of theses, including Bachelor Theses, Master Theses, Master Projects, and Independent Studies. When you begin a thesis with BDLT, you will work under the supervision of the group chair, Professor Claudio J. Tessone. Additionally, a co-supervisor will be assigned to you as your primary contact person.

The list below provides the titles, types, and designated co-supervisors for each thesis. Please choose the thesis topic that interests you and reach out to the corresponding co-supervisor for further information.

If your desired topic is not currently listed, please feel free to reach out to any BDLT member for an informal meeting. They might have new ideas that are not yet included in the list or may be open to considering your own suggestions.

 
Abbreviations utilized below read as follows:
  • BA: Bachelor Thesis
  • MA: Master Thesis
  • IS:  Independent Study
  • MAP: Master Project

Notes

Each type of thesis work (BA, IS, MAP, or MA) must adhere to its own set of structural and content requirements. This ensures that the resulting thesis meets a high standard of quality when presented in written form, showcasing the student's effort, input, and dedication.
