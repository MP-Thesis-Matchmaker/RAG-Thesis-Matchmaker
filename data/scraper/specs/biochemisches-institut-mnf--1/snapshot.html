Biochemistry Master's Program

Biochemistry Master's Program

The master's degree program in biochemistry builds up on a bachelor’s degree in biochemistry. The program imparts guiding knowledge in structural biology, protein engineering and biochemistry. Sound experimental competencies are developed within a research project and the Master’s thesis. In the area of generic competencies the abilities to transfer concepts, to describe problems and to formulate hypotheses are promoted. The master’s degree in biochemistry is the professional qualification for research activities in the life sciences.
